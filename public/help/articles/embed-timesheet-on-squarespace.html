How to embed your public timesheet on Squarespace

Last updated June 2026

Show your weekly hours on your Squarespace site. You copy a small piece of code from Roozfir and paste it into your page.

  1. In Roozfir, click Embeds in the left-hand menu.
  2. Under What do you want to embed?, choose Public timesheet. The preview on the right shows what visitors will see.
  3. Choose a Theme (Light or Dark) and a Size.
  4. Click Copy code. A message confirms the code is on your clipboard.
  5. In Squarespace, open the page and click Edit.
  6. Hover where you want the timesheet, click Add Block (the + sign) and choose Code.
  7. Paste the code into the box. Make sure Display Source is turned off.
  8. Click Apply, then Save.

Tip: Code blocks need a plan that allows them. If you do not see the Code block, check your Squarespace plan.
