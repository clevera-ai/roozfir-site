How to embed your public timesheet on Google Sites

Last updated June 2026

Show your weekly hours on your Google Sites site. You copy a small piece of code from Roozfir and paste it into your page.

  1. In Roozfir, click Embeds in the left-hand menu.
  2. Under What do you want to embed?, choose Public timesheet. The preview on the right shows what visitors will see.
  3. Choose a Theme (Light or Dark) and a Size.
  4. Click Copy code. A message confirms the code is on your clipboard.
  5. In Google Sites, open your site and the page you want to change.
  6. On the right, open the Insert tab and click Embed.
  7. Choose Embed code at the top of the window, paste the code, and click Next.
  8. Click Insert, resize the box if needed, and click Publish.
