How to connect Slack

Last updated April 2026

Connect Slack so Roozfir can post project and invoice news to a channel.

  1. Click Integrations in the left-hand menu.
  2. Find the Slack card and click Connect.
  3. Slack opens in a new screen and asks you to sign in and allow Roozfir to post messages to your channels. Choose Allow.
  4. You land back in Roozfir. The Slack card now says Connected.
