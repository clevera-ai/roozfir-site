How to connect Xero

Last updated May 2026

Connect Xero so your invoices can be sent to your accounts automatically.

  1. Click Integrations in the left-hand menu.
  2. Find the Xero card and click Connect.
  3. Xero opens in a new screen and asks you to sign in and allow Roozfir to create invoices in your organisation. Choose Allow.
  4. You land back in Roozfir. The Xero card now says Connected.
