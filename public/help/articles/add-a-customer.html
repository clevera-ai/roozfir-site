How to add a customer

Last updated January 2026

Customers are the people and companies you bill. You add one before you can create projects and invoices for them.

  1. Click Contacts in the left-hand menu, then Customers.
  2. Click Add customer.
  3. Enter the Name and Email. The main contact and hourly rate are optional; the rate is used when you turn tracked time into invoice lines.
  4. Click Save customer.

Tip: Have a spreadsheet? Use Import customers on the same page to bring in a CSV file.
