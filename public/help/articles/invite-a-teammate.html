How to invite a teammate and choose their role

Last updated February 2026

Everyone you invite gets a role that decides what they can see and change.

  1. Open Team in the left-hand menu.
  2. Click Invite someone.
  3. Enter their Email address and choose a Role: Admin, Manager, Member or Viewer.
  4. Click Send invitation. They show as "Invited" until they accept.

Tip: To see exactly what each role can do, open Permissions at the top of the Team page.
