Frequently asked questions

Last updated November 2026

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Click a question to open it.

Can I use Roozfir on my phone?

Yes. The menu folds into a button at the top left on small screens and everything else works the same.

Why is my invoice number not what I expected?

Numbers start with the prefix you chose during setup, then count up. See the invoice numbering guide for how to change the prefix.

Does Roozfir round my time?

Only to the nearest minute when you stop the timer. Read How Roozfir rounds time for the details.

How do I give someone else access?

Click People, then Add teammate. You choose a role, and you can fine-tune what each role can do under Permissions.

Which plan do I need for Xero and workflows?

Both need the Business plan. See Set up Xero sync and Build a workflow.

I see a cookie message on every visit.

Choose Accept all, Reject all or Customise once and we remember it on this device. You can change it later in Settings, Privacy.

Where is my data stored?

Roozfir is a demo product. Everything you enter is kept in your own browser and can be cleared at any time.
