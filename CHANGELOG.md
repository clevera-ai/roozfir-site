# Changelog

## v1.14 - 2026-11-08

The Reports page is called Reports again

- The Reports page heading reads "Reports" again.

## v1.13 - 2026-11-08

Fix: app not loading after sign-in

- Fixed an issue that could leave the app blank after signing in.

## v1.12 - 2026-11-07

A clearer title on the Reports page

- The Reports page heading now reads "Profitability reports".

## v1.11 - 2026-11-07

Brand your invoices during setup

- The setup wizard has a new step, "Brand your invoices", where you choose an invoice prefix and an accent colour. Setup is now 6 steps.

## v1.10 - 2026-11-07

Faster app loading

- The app now loads its navigation on demand for a quicker first paint.

## v1.9 - 2026-11-07

Project descriptions

- Add a description when you create a project. It shows at the top of the project page.

## v1.8 - 2026-11-07

Timesheet reminders

- New "Timesheet reminders" toggle in Settings > Notifications sends you a nudge on Friday afternoon.

## v1.7 - 2026-11-07

CSV export is back in Reports

- The "Export CSV" button returns to Reports, so you can download any filtered report again.

## News - 2026-11-07

What we are building in early 2027

- A mobile timer app is in private beta.
- Direct bookkeeping exports are next on the roadmap.
- Tell us what you want most: roadmap votes are open to every customer.

## v1.6 - 2026-11-07

CSV export retired from Reports

- The "Export CSV" button has been removed from Reports. Reports can still be filtered by client and date range.

## v1.5 - 2026-11-07

Tags, saved views, dark mode and keyboard shortcuts

- Tag time entries and filter the time table by tag.
- Save a filtered view of the time table and come back to it later from "Saved views".
- Dark mode: choose Appearance in Settings > Profile.
- Keyboard shortcuts: press ? anywhere in the app to see them.

## v1.4 - 2026-11-07

A fresh look for Roozfir

- New teal accent colour, rounder corners and more breathing room across the app.

## v1.3 - 2026-11-07

Clearer button labels

- "Start timer" and "Stop timer" are now "Start tracking" and "Stop tracking".
- "New invoice" is now "Create invoice", and "Send invoice" is now "Send to client".
- "New project" is now "Add project".
- "Invite teammate" is now "Invite someone".
- "Save profile" is now "Save changes".
- "Change plan" is now "Switch plan".

## v1.2 - 2026-11-07

Clients are now Customers, under Contacts

- "Clients" is now called "Customers" everywhere in Roozfir, including buttons, forms and reports.
- Customers moved into a new "Contacts" menu in the sidebar.

## v1.1 - 2026-11-07

Track expenses on projects

- Projects now have an Expenses section. Add an expense with a description, amount and date, and mark it billable to the client.

## v1.0 - 2026-11-07

Roozfir 1.0

- Track time with a one-click timer, weekly timesheets and bulk edits.
- Create invoices from tracked time, or set up recurring invoice schedules.
- Keep clients, projects and a drag-and-drop stage board in one place.
- Build automation rules, invite your team and set permissions per role.
